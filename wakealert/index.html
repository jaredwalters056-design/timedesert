How it works

Three steps, then it does the rest

Set it up once. After that WakeAlert runs the whole morning on its own.

Set your alarm and your wake window

Choose a sound, a snooze length, and how long WakeAlert should keep checking on you after you silence it.

The alarm goes off

Your phone sounds and your Watch taps. Depending on your settings, you may have to complete a wake challenge to silence it.

WakeAlert keeps watching

Through your wake window it tracks wrist motion and heart rate. Fall back asleep and it rings again. Stay up and it quietly logs the morning to your history.
